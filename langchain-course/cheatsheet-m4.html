LangGraph agent

b.add_edge(START, "assistant")
b.add_conditional_edges("assistant", tools_condition)

g = b.compile(checkpointer=InMemorySaver(), interrupt_before=["tools"])

Common mistakes

  • Caching personal answers across users.
  • No timeouts or retries.
  • Copying 2023 tutorials without converting.
  • No recursion limit on custom graphs.
