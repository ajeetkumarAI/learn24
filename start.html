New to AI? Start here.

Learn24 teaches how modern AI works with pictures, animations and small live demos. You do not need a maths degree. This page tells you what you need, where to begin and how to use each lesson.

What you need

🧮

School-level maths

If you know multiplication, averages and percentages, you can follow every lesson. Formulas are always explained in words first.

🐍

Basic Python (for code parts)

Only for the code boxes: variables, lists, functions and pip install. New to Python? Try the free official tutorial first.

💻

A free place to run code

Google Colab runs Python in your browser with a free GPU. Some examples call OpenAI and need an API key; the lessons say when.

⏱

20–30 minutes a day

Each lesson takes 5–15 minutes of reading, plus time to try the code. One lesson a day finishes the GenAI Course in about a month.

Five words to know before you begin

TokenA small piece of text (a word or part of a word). Models read and write tokens.
EmbeddingA list of numbers that stands for meaning. Similar meanings → similar numbers.
AttentionHow each word looks at the other words to understand context.
PromptThe text and instructions you send to the model.
RAGSearch your documents first, then let the model answer from them.

See all 83 terms in the Glossary. Inside lessons, words with a dotted underline show their meaning when you hover or tap them.

How each lesson works

  1. Animated lessons play step by step. They start at 0.5× speed; change it to 0.25×–2×, pause, or click Next to go at your own pace.
  2. Article lessons have diagrams that appear as you scroll, small demos you can click, and code you can copy.
  3. Hover or tap underlined words to see their meaning without leaving the page.
  4. Take the quiz at the end of each lesson, then press Mark as done. Your progress is saved in this browser.
  5. Revise with cheat sheets: every GenAI Course module has a one-page summary you can print.

Learning from outside India?

Many examples use Indian banking and daily life: UPI (instant mobile payments), KYC (identity checks), NEFT/IMPS (bank transfers), ₹ (rupee) and lakh (100,000). They are all in the Glossary. To read a lesson in your language, use your browser’s Translate option; the diagrams and demos still work.
