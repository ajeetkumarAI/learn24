Transformer Variants

⏱ 12 min read🎯 Intermediate🖼 2 diagrams🧪 1 live demo🐍 Python code

The original transformer had both an encoder and a decoder. Since then, researchers found that for many jobs you need only one half. This gave us three big families. Once you understand them, choosing a model for any task becomes simple.

In this session

  • The three families: encoder-only, decoder-only and encoder-decoder
  • How each family is pre-trained
  • Key models: BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT, DeBERTa · GPT family · T5, BART
  • A decision guide for picking the right family for a task

01Three families from one design

🎓

Think of three jobs in an office. The encoder is the reader who understands and sorts documents. The decoder is the writer who drafts new letters. The encoder-decoder is the translator who reads one document and writes a new version of it.

02Encoder-only models

These are the BERT family. Every token sees every other token, so they build rich understanding. They are small, fast and cheap, perfect for classification, search and extraction. We covered them in depth in the BERT lesson.

ModelKey ideaSize (base)
BERT (2018)Masked language modelling + next sentence prediction110 M
RoBERTa (2019)No NSP, more data, longer training125 M
DistilBERT (2019)Distilled from BERT: 40% smaller, 60% faster66 M
DeBERTa (2020)Separate content and position attention~140 M

03Decoder-only models: the GPT family

Decoder-only models drop the encoder and cross-attention. They learn one simple game on huge text: predict the next token. Scale this up and you get the chat models we use every day.

Almost every modern LLM is decoder-only: GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek and Gemma. Why did this design win?

  • One simple objective that works on any text, so training data is unlimited.
  • Any task can be phrased as text generation: classification, translation, coding and Q&A all become “continue this text”.
  • Scales very well: bigger models and more data keep getting better.

04Encoder-decoder models: T5 and BART

These keep both towers. They shine when the output is a new version of the input: translation, summarisation, rewriting.

  • T5 (Google, 2019) treats every task as text-to-text. You add a prefix like summarize: or translate English to German: and it writes the answer. It is pre-trained with span corruption: random spans of text are replaced with placeholders and the model writes the missing spans. Flan-T5 is an instruction-tuned version.
  • BART (Facebook, 2019) is pre-trained as a denoising autoencoder: text is damaged (words masked, deleted or shuffled) and the model learns to rebuild the original. It is especially good at summarisation.
FamilyPre-training gameExample
Encoder-onlyFill in masked tokens“my card is [MASK]” → blocked
Decoder-onlyPredict the next token“my card is” → blocked
Encoder-decoder (T5)Rebuild missing spans“my <X> blocked” → <X> card is
Encoder-decoder (BART)Repair damaged text“card my blocked is” → my card is blocked

05Choosing the right family

If your task is…Start withWhy
Classify, tag, extract, rankEncoder-onlySmall, fast, accurate after fine-tuning
Chat, write, code, reasonDecoder-onlyStrong generation and instruction following
Translate, summarise, rewriteEncoder-decoder (or decoder-only)Built for input → new output
Search and RAG retrievalEncoder embedding modelFast vectors for similarity

06Code: one family each, with pipelines

three_families.py
from transformers import pipeline

# Encoder-only: fill a blank
fill = pipeline("fill-mask", model="distilbert-base-uncased")
print(fill("My debit card is [MASK].")[0]["token_str"])

# Decoder-only: continue text
gen = pipeline("text-generation", model="gpt2")
print(gen("Artificial intelligence is", max_new_tokens=15)[0]["generated_text"])

# Encoder-decoder: summarise
summ = pipeline("summarization", model="facebook/bart-large-cnn")
text = "The bank launched a new app that lets customers block cards, check balances and raise complaints in Hindi, Marathi and English. It also sends instant alerts for every transaction."
print(summ(text, max_length=30, min_length=10)[0]["summary_text"])
Outputs vary with model versions and sampling, so we don’t show fixed outputs here. Run it in Colab and compare what each family produces.

07Summary

  • Encoder-only (BERT family) = understanding: classify, extract, search.
  • Decoder-only (GPT family and most LLMs) = generation: chat, write, code.
  • Encoder-decoder (T5, BART) = transformation: translate, summarise, rewrite.
  • Pick by task: small encoders are often cheaper and better for narrow understanding tasks.
