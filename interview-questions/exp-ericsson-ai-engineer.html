About this interview

Compiled from a public Ericsson AI Engineer report (Tirupati campus, November 2025: one interview focused on your projects and the technologies behind them) plus context from Ericsson's telecom-AI work. The questions are paraphrased, and the model answers are written by Learn24.

Role AI Engineer (graduate / campus)
Rounds One interview: project deep dive → probing the technologies used
Outcome Not selected; the candidate struggled to articulate details clearly
Domain Network AI: anomaly detection, traffic forecasting, energy saving, intent-based networks, GenAI for operations

01Rounds at a glance

  • Project + technology depth: if you list LSTM, Docker or FastAPI, expect "how does it work and why did you choose it?"
  • Communication counts: practise explaining technical details clearly and simply.

02Questions and model answers

Q1
You used an LSTM in your project. Explain how it works and why you chose it.
  • LSTM cell: a cell state (long-term memory) plus three gates:
  • Forget gate: what to drop.
  • Input gate: what new information to add.
  • Output gate: what to expose as the hidden state.
  • Why it helps: the additive cell-state updates let gradients flow over long sequences, which avoids the vanishing gradients of plain RNNs.
  • Why I chose it: "Network KPI time series with daily and weekly patterns; the LSTM beat ARIMA by 12% MAPE." Also mention that Temporal CNNs or Transformers are alternatives, and why you didn't use them (data size, latency).
Q2
How would you detect anomalies in network KPIs (latency, packet loss, throughput)?
  • Baselines: seasonal decomposition (STL) and residual z-scores per cell or site; EWMA control charts.
  • ML: Isolation Forest on multivariate KPIs; LSTM/Transformer forecasters, flagging large residuals; autoencoders on KPI vectors.
  • Practicalities: a different seasonality per site, alert fatigue (aggregate and correlate alarms), root-cause hints (neighbouring cells, config changes).
  • Evaluate with labelled incidents (precision and recall of alerts, time to detect).
Q3
You used Docker. What problem does it solve, and how is it different from a VM?
  • Docker packages an app with its dependencies into an image; containers run it consistently anywhere ("works on my machine" solved).
  • vs VM: containers share the host OS kernel, so they are lightweight and start in seconds. VMs virtualise the hardware with a full guest OS, giving stronger isolation but at a heavier cost.
  • In ML: reproducible training and serving environments, easy deployment to Kubernetes.
Q4
How can GenAI help telecom network operations?
  • NOC copilot: summarise alarms and incidents, suggest likely root causes from past tickets (RAG), draft change plans.
  • Natural-language intent → configuration: translate operator intents into validated configuration templates (with human approval and simulation).
  • Knowledge assistants over 3GPP standards and vendor documents.
  • Guardrails: never push config changes without validation; audit trails; strict access control.
Q5
How do you explain your project clearly in an interview?

Answer (3-minute template):

  1. One sentence: "I built an LSTM model that forecasts cell-tower traffic 24 hours ahead to save energy."
  2. Data: source, size, preprocessing (two sentences).
  3. Model: why LSTM, its key parameters, the baseline comparison.
  4. Result: "MAPE 8.4% against 9.6% for ARIMA, enabling sleep mode at off-peak sites."
  5. Your role and learnings. - Rehearse aloud and record yourself. Clarity was the deciding factor in this report.
Q6
What is 5G network slicing, and where might ML help?
  • Slicing: logically separate virtual networks on shared infrastructure, each with guaranteed characteristics (e.g. low-latency for industrial IoT, high-bandwidth for video).
  • ML uses: forecasting slice demand, dynamic resource allocation (RL), SLA violation prediction, anomaly detection per slice.

03How to prepare for Ericsson

  • Know every technology on your resume deeply (LSTM, Docker, frameworks).
  • Practise a crisp project pitch, and learn telecom-AI basics (KPI anomaly detection, forecasting, slicing).
