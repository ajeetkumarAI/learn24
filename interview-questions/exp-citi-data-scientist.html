02Questions and model answers

Q1
How does a decision tree decide where to split? How do you stop it overfitting?
  • Classification: choose the feature and threshold that maximise impurity reduction.
  • Gini = 1 − Σpᵢ², or entropy = −Σpᵢ log pᵢ, weighted by child sizes (information gain).
  • Regression: minimise the variance (MSE) of the children.
  • Greedy and recursive: it continues until a stopping criterion is met.
  • Control overfitting:
  • max_depth, min_samples_leaf, min_samples_split.
  • Cost-complexity pruning (ccp_alpha).
  • Or ensembles: random forests (bagging) and gradient boosting.
  • In banking: shallow trees and scorecards are favoured for explainability to regulators.
Q2
Design a RAG system for internal banking policies and procedures.
  • Ingestion: policy PDFs and SOPs → layout-aware parsing → clause-level chunks with metadata (policy ID, version, effective date, business unit, classification) → embeddings → vector DB, plus BM25.
  • Access control: entitlement filters at retrieval (users see only the policies for their business unit and classification).
  • Query: rewrite → hybrid search → cross-encoder re-rank → latest effective version only → grounded answer with clause citations.
  • Guardrails:
  • No financial advice to customers.
  • PII redaction.
  • "Not found" when retrieval confidence is low.
  • Prompt-injection filtering.
  • Governance:
  • A model inventory entry and validation (model risk management).
  • An evaluation set signed off by compliance.
  • Audit logs, periodic review, and data residency on a private model endpoint.
Q3
What is a vector database and how would you choose one for a bank?
  • What it does: stores embeddings with ANN indexes (HNSW, IVF-PQ) for fast similarity search, plus metadata filtering.
  • Bank criteria:
  • On-premises or private-cloud deployment.
  • Encryption at rest and in transit.
  • RBAC and attribute filters.
  • Audit logs.
  • High availability and backups.
  • Scale (millions of chunks).
  • Hybrid search.
  • Vendor approval.
  • Options: pgvector (inside an approved Postgres), Elasticsearch/OpenSearch (often already approved), Milvus, Qdrant (self-hosted), Azure AI Search with private endpoints.
Q4
Explain batch normalisation and why it helps.
  • For each mini-batch, normalise the activations to mean 0 and variance 1, then apply a learned scale γ and shift β.
  • Benefits:
  • Stabilises the distribution of layer inputs.
  • Allows higher learning rates and faster convergence.
  • Reduces sensitivity to initialisation.
  • Has a mild regularising effect.
  • Inference: uses running averages of the mean and variance.
  • Limitations: depends on batch size (small batches hurt), so Transformers use LayerNorm.
Q5
How would you optimise a reinforcement-learning algorithm that trains slowly or unstably?
  • Stability:
  • Target networks and experience replay (DQN).
  • Clipped objectives (PPO).
  • Gradient clipping.
  • Normalised observations and rewards.
  • Entropy bonus for exploration.
  • Sample efficiency:
  • Prioritised replay.
  • Off-policy algorithms (SAC, TD3).
  • Reward shaping (carefully).
  • Imitation learning or pre-training on logged data.
  • Speed: vectorised and parallel environments, a smaller network, tuned hyperparameters (learning rate, γ, batch size), and tracking learning curves over several seeds.
  • Banking use cases: dynamic pricing, collections strategy, trade execution. Mention offline RL and the need for safe policy evaluation.
Q6
Case-study presentation: how do you structure a 15-minute data-science case presentation for senior stakeholders?
  1. Executive summary first (1 slide): the problem, the recommendation, the impact.
  2. Context and data (2 slides).
  3. Approach (2–3 slides): keep it simple, explain why.
  4. Results (2 slides): metrics and business impact in money terms.
  5. Risks and next steps (1 slide): limitations, monitoring, governance. - Leave time for questions. Executives probe assumptions and risk, not hyperparameters.

03How to prepare for Citi

  • Revise classic ML (trees, ensembles) and DL basics (batch norm, optimisers).
  • Prepare a bank-grade RAG design with entitlements, citations and model risk governance.
  • Expect an aptitude and data-interpretation online test in India.
