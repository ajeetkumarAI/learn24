Lab 8: Final Readiness Drill

⏱ 10 min read🧪 Hands-on lab☁️ Microsoft Foundry

The last lab: solve topic-wise practice sets against the clock, attempt Mock Exam 3 aiming above 80%, fill in the readiness scorecard for every exam skill, and pick and book your AI-901 exam date on Pearson VUE.

Time: 2–3 hours · You need: the AI-901 Exam Practice page.

Outcome. Exam-ready confidence and a booked exam date.

Step 1: Timed topic sets

On Topic practice, switch to timed mode and complete every topic set. Give yourself about 50 seconds per question. Record each score.

Step 2: Mock Exam 3

Take Mock Exam 3 in exam mode and target 80%+ (the equivalent of 800/1000). Review it fully.

Step 3: Readiness scorecard

Rate each skill from the official outline: ✅ confident (≥ 80% and done hands-on), 🟡 nearly (60–79%), ❌ not yet.

Any ❌ → revise that lesson and redo its lab step before booking.

Step 4: Book your exam

  1. On the AI-901 exam page on Microsoft Learn, select Schedule exam and sign in with your personal Microsoft account.
  2. Check that your profile name matches your ID.
  3. Choose Pearson VUE (online or test centre), pick a date within the next 2–3 weeks, while everything is fresh, and complete payment.
  4. Add the date to your calendar along with the last-week plan from Session 14.

Step 5: Earn your Learn24 certificate

Finish the course lessons and labs (press Mark as done on each page) and pass Mock Exam 3 on the practice page. Then open Certificates to download your Azure AI-901 Exam Ready certificate and add it to LinkedIn.

Check yourself

How long before the exam should you book? Soon after you are consistently at 80%+, typically 2–3 weeks ahead, so the knowledge stays fresh and the date keeps you accountable.
